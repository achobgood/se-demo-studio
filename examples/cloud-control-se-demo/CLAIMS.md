## 2. Shown, not claimed

The footage demonstrates these; the narration describes them only as what happens on screen.

- Home lists top actions and a prompt box; the top bar shows Intersight, Nexus Hyperfabric, Security,
  Collaboration Control Hub, Meraki, IQ, ThousandEyes, and "+2".
- Searching `Mexico` in Actions returns the Sept 20 action tagged SD-WAN and Meraki.
- Opening it starts an Assistant analysis on screen ("Analyzing...", "Network Platform Agent: ...").
- The analysis reports "Checked insights from: the action record, Catalyst SD-WAN telemetry, Meraki
  Dashboard API." (basis for "It's reading the action record, the Catalyst SD-WAN telemetry, and the
  Meraki data.").
- Related alerts: many SD-WAN rows on San Jose edges (SanJose-Branch1-Edge1/Edge2,
  SanJose-DC2-Edge1/Edge2) and Meraki "Unreachable device" rows for CW9162-AP-2 and C9200-Access-1.
- The Analysis separates a San Jose SD-WAN control-plane event (with UTD security-service
  disruption) from Mexico City devices that "became unreachable independently during the same
  window", with an AP "power event/reboot".
- The Default answer: switch online, CW9162-AP-2 offline although its alert was marked resolved, and
  "No MX/VMX security appliance is present ... WAN uplink health for this site cannot be validated
  from the Meraki side".
- Collaborate in Canvas opens a canvas that carries the action's conversation.
- The Reasoning answer and its card: both APs and the switch unreachable "at overlapping times",
  "site-wide upstream connectivity problem", top cause "Upstream WAN/Cloud Connectivity Loss", and
  the same MX visibility gap.
- The footer "Assistant can make mistakes. Verify responses." (close-up 3:34 to 3:43).
- ThousandEyes opens from the same top bar; MEXICO-UPLINK-PUBLIC-Inet is alerting, loss is 100%
  across Sep 24, and both Mexico City edges' paths stop at 10.255.0.3 before 198.18.14.1.
- Generate summary produces "Network Connectivity Incident Report: San Jose and Mexico City".
- Share shows "Invite link copied to clipboard."

## 4. Deliberately not said

- **No numbers as claims.** No time savings, MTTR, percentages, customer or waitlist counts (S2
  "More than 300 customers ..."), integration counts ("more than 60"), the S2 "10 hours to under 10
  minutes" anecdote, the S3 "four-hour incident into a forty-minute one" hypothetical, or demo counts
  (8 search results, 66 related alerts, six devices, alert totals, "4 days").
- **No root cause as certainty.** Never "finds the root cause". The narration says "first pass",
  "separates", "points to", "top candidate", "good lead", and "verify".
- **No autonomous fixing.** No claim that the AI or agents change the network; "trusted" or
  autonomous agents are future tense (S10). Change goes through reviewed workflows a person runs.
- **No workflow shown or run**, and no suggestion that workflows run inside Reasoning (S5: Default
  mode only).
- **Controllers stay.** Never "replaces" controllers, consoles, or dashboards; never "single pane of
  glass".
- **Not "free" and not "for every customer".** Always "included at no additional cost with eligible
  Cisco subscriptions", with Essential and Advantage tiers, US scope, and "Cisco may change this" in
  fine print.
- **Not "one product is enough".** The contested single-domain entry (S3 vs S13 and S4) stays out; the
  narration says "start with those" and "every product you connect adds context".
- **Not Security Cloud Control.** The "Security" tab is visible but never narrated.
- **No beta, planned, or in-development items.** No Workflows agent (beta), Agent Studio, Cloud
  Control Studio or its third-party connections, Marketplace, the main-menu Apps list, Secure Access
  (Beta) agent, Catalyst Center Controlled Availability versions, or SD-WAN Cloud-Pro.
- **No regions beyond the US.** No "global", "worldwide", or "everywhere".
- **No "sees everything"** and nothing about the traffic path.
- **No Assign, Resolve, plan-review, or "Investigation limitations" claims.** All are documented (S4,
  S5) but did not appear in this build or run.
- **No invented SE experience or customer stories.** Customer questions are framed "a fair question
  here is"; no logos, quotes, or testimonials.
- **No "request access".** GA next-step wording only.
